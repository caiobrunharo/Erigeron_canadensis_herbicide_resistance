Note on file extensions
=======================

To get past the mail server's attachment filter, three script files were given
a trailing ".txt" extension. After unzipping, rename them back by removing the
".txt":

  04_figures/te_figures.py.txt                 ->  te_figures.py
  03_annotate_families/add_transposon_family.py.txt  ->  add_transposon_family.py
  02_run_temp2/run_TEMP2.slurm.txt             ->  run_TEMP2.slurm

One-liner to restore them all (run from inside transposable_elements/):

  find . -name '*.txt' ! -name 'prefixes.txt' ! -name 'RESTORE_FILENAMES.txt' \
    -exec sh -c 'mv "$1" "${1%.txt}"' _ {} \;

(prefixes.txt and the .md/.yml files are genuine text/config files and should
be left as-is.)
